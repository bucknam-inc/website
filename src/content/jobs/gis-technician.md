**Full-time or part-time** · Oceanside, CA

Bucknam Infrastructure Group, Inc. is seeking a GIS Technician to support our growing portfolio of municipal GIS, pavement-management, and right-of-way projects across Southern California.

### Description & Duties

The GIS Technician will build, digitize, maintain, and publish spatial data for city, county, and private-agency clients. Day-to-day work covers dataset creation in ArcGIS Pro, web-map publishing to ArcGIS Online, asset feature collection in the field, and quality control of deliverables produced by our infrastructure-management team.

### Minimum Qualifications

- Associate's or Bachelor's degree in Geography, GIS, Civil Engineering, or a related field — **or** equivalent work experience
- **One or more years** of hands-on GIS work using ESRI products (ArcGIS Pro, ArcGIS Online)
- Valid **California Driver's License**

### Essential Functions

- Digitize and maintain spatial datasets (parcels, streets, signs, striping, sidewalks, ADA ramps, hydrants, etc.) from aerial imagery, GPS, and field notes
- Build and publish web maps and web apps in ArcGIS Online for client use
- Support field collection of assets with GPS-enabled devices and digital imagery workflows
- Perform QA/QC on GIS data produced by project teams
- Produce map exhibits, reports, and data packages for client deliverables
- Convert data between formats (shapefile, GeoJSON, CSV, file geodatabase) and integrate with client systems
- Assist with pavement-management program data loads (MicroPAVER, StreetSaver, CarteGraph)
- Collaborate with the pavement and ROW inventory teams to keep GIS data aligned with field observations

### Desired Skills & Experience

- Proficiency with **ESRI ArcGIS Pro** and **ArcGIS Online**
- Working knowledge of Python or Arcade for ArcGIS scripting and field calculations
- Experience with mobile data collection (ArcGIS Field Maps, Survey123, Collector)
- Local agency (City, County) GIS experience preferred
- Familiarity with municipal asset management — pavement, ROW, utility, or facility datasets
- Strong attention to detail and the ability to perform repetitive digitizing tasks with high accuracy
- Ability to work independently in the field and collaboratively in the office
- Clean driving record

### Nice to Have

- Experience with MicroPAVER, MTC StreetSaver, or other pavement-management platforms
- FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate for drone/UAV data capture
- Web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) for customizing ArcGIS web apps
