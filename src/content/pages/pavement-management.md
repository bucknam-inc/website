## Our Approach

With more than twenty years of professional experience managing pavement networks in Southern California, our team brings relevant, qualified, and proven success to your project. Pavement management isn't simply identifying condition — it is knowing how to manage a network in a way that benefits short-term needs and long-term goals.

Bucknam stands at the forefront of pavement management implementation, assessment, and delivery. Our project manager (and President), Mr. Peter Bucknam, has managed over **240 PMP projects** throughout the western United States; his teams combined have surveyed over **65,500 miles of pavement** over the past 25 years. That experience lends to applicable solutions for your unique pavement needs and goals.

## Local Agency Expertise

Bucknam's pavement management team brings expertise demonstrated through:

- Experience managing pavement projects for over twenty years
- Assisting cities comply with **LACMTA**, **OCTA Measure M2**, and other PMP requirements
- Implementing numerous pavement management software packages — **MicroPAVER**, **StreetSaver**, **CarteGraph**, and more
- Extensive PMP project management across Orange, Los Angeles, San Diego, Ventura, and the Inland Empire
- Using **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** to create road segment polygons for improved asphalt true-area calculations
- Public-works experience from the *city* side — former City Engineer and Public Works Director roles inform our methodology
- A realistic, proactive, and repeatable PMP methodology that matches your agency's needs and goals

Bucknam builds on our knowledge and understanding of your PMP goals. Mr. Bucknam's team includes dedicated, certified, and qualified managers and field technicians who have served under his management for over twenty years on PMP projects. Our inspectors update your PMP through sound **Army Corps of Engineers ASTM D6433 (streets)** and **D5340 (airport)** inspection methodologies.
