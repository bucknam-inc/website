Do you use Pavement Management Software to track your road condition? Do you wish you had a convenient way to access and filter your data?
Introducing our new MyRoads App we built to help you visualize your Pavement Management information.This application allows users to filter by Network, Zone, Rank, Branch and PCI value. In addition to giving historical data for the selected features the application has a calculator to help estimate repair projects.
[Click here](https://bucknam-inc.com/myroads) to learn more!
